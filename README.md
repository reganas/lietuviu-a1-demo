# Lietuvių kalba A1 — demo

Two A1 Lithuanian grammar practice apps, free to use:
**https://reganas.github.io/lietuviu-a1-demo/**

- **1 skyrius — Kas tu esi?** personal pronouns, *būti / nebūti*, *kas* and *ar*
- **2 skyrius — Iš kur tu esi?** *iš* + genitive, present tense (-a type), adverbs in *-iškai*

Each app declares the last coursebook unit whose grammar may appear, and nothing
on the page runs past it. Everything the learner is taught is Lithuanian at that
level; every instruction, hint and piece of feedback is in English, because
Lithuanian rubrics quietly introduce grammar the learner has not met yet.

One HTML file. No install, no account, no sign-in — open the link on a phone or a
laptop and it works.
